Tailwind px → vw Conversion Guide
🎯 Goal
Make sure to use vw unit insted of px or rem etc. Base viewport: 1333px (so 1px = 0.075vw).
📐 Core Formula
Tailwind spacing unit × 0.3 = vw
(e.g. w-8 → w-[2.4vw], because 8 × 0.3 = 2.4)
Raw px × 0.075 = vw
(e.g. 16px → 1.2vw)

🔄 Arbitrary Value Conversion
When you encounter arbitrary px values, convert directly:
w-[20px] → w-[1.5vw] (20 × 0.075)
p-[10px] → p-[0.75vw] (10 × 0.075)
top-[100px] → top-[7.5vw] (100 × 0.075)
text-[18px] → text-[1.35vw] (18 × 0.075)
For rem: multiply by 1.2 (since 1rem = 16px = 1.2vw).
w-[1.5rem] → w-[1.8vw] (1.5 × 1.2)
