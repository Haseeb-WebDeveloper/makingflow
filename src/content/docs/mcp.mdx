Connect an AI assistant to your workspace over the Model Context Protocol. It can build forms,
publish them, read responses and answer questions about how they are performing — from whichever
tool you already work in.

## Two ways to connect, and which one you need [#connecting]

It depends entirely on your client, so it is worth getting right before you start.

**The chat assistants sign in with your MakingFlow account.** There is nothing to create on our
side — you add the URL above as a custom connector in their settings, and they send you here to
choose what they may reach. They have nowhere to put an API key, so making one for them would not
help.

**Everything else uses a key.** Create one in Integrations, choosing its permissions and
workspaces, then paste it into your client's config. A key can cover several workspaces, so you set
it up once.

## Connecting with a key [#api-key]

Open **Integrations** in MakingFlow, choose **Connect**, and pick your app. The key is shown
**once** — we store only a one-way hash, so it cannot be recovered. If you lose it, revoke it and
make another.

## Permissions [#permissions]

<McpPermissionsTable />

Whichever way you connect, a connection only ever sees the tools its permissions allow, and can
never do more than the person who set it up — its role is re-read from your workspace on every
request, so removing someone cuts their connections off immediately.

Deleting forms and responses is never offered when you connect. It stays off by design, and the
deletion tools additionally require an explicit confirmation on every call.

## Disconnecting [#disconnecting]

**Integrations → View details** lists everything connected, whichever way it was set up, and
disconnects any of it. That takes effect on the connection's very next request — not whenever a
token happens to expire.

## A note on responses [#prompt-injection]

<Callout variant="security">
Response content is written by the people filling in your forms. When an assistant reads it, treat
it as data to report on — not as instructions to act on.
</Callout>
